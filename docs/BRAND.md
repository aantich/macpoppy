# Poppy website identity

Poppy is the umbrella brand for Deep, MagicBox, and Velvet. The landing page's headline is **“Make more of your Mac.”** Manrope is the selected typeface.

## Logos and icons

The Poppy flower uses coral-red petals and a plum center. All live flower placements and the favicon use [the transparent PNG](../assets/poppy-mark-transparent.png). Its original reference and full-size transparent export are preserved in `assets/source/`.

The product icons follow a consistent Mac-style design: crisp, mostly flat shapes; modest gradients; and matching rounded app tiles with transparent outer corners.

| Product | Symbol | Editable asset |

| Deep | Teal tile, white shield, backup arrow | [SVG](../assets/icons/deep.svg) |
| MagicBox | Coral tile, white toolbox, small sparkle | [SVG](../assets/icons/magicbox.svg) |

## Typography

Use Manrope Medium 500 for brand and product names, with Regular 400 for supporting copy. Product body copy is 18px on desktop and 16px on phones. Keep app descriptions readable, and let the headlines carry the display scale.

The [Manrope variable font](../assets/fonts/Manrope-Variable.ttf), [license](../assets/fonts/OFL.txt), and [source information](../assets/fonts/SOURCE.txt) are included locally.

## Product language

- **Poppy Deep:** backup and security for Mac.
- **Poppy MagicBox:** disk-space analysis, archive tools, system monitoring, clipboard history, and window layouts in one app.
- **Poppy Velvet:** Linux virtualization, containers, and developer workflows on Mac.

The products are in development. Interface examples are concepts; avoid implying current availability, tested compatibility, or measured performance.

This is the website repository's portable identity summary. The umbrella workspace maintains the complete brand guide and original monochrome explorations separately.

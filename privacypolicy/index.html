Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

This policy covers macpoppy.com and the current versions of Poppy MagicBox and Poppy Velvet.

Who is responsible

Anton Antich operates Poppy as an individual and is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@macpoppy.com.

Website and analytics

GitHub Pages hosts macpoppy.com and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Cloudflare provides DNS for the domain. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

We use Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors find and use the site. Google Analytics can receive information about the pages you visit, where you came from, your browser and device, and how you interact with the site. It can use cookies or similar identifiers to measure visits. Google’s services receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser connects to them. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can manage or delete cookies in your browser and limit Google Analytics measurement with Google’s opt-out browser add-on. We keep analytics data according to the service’s retention settings for the time needed to review site usage and trends.

Poppy MagicBox

Monitor reads system resource usage and, where available, usage by running processes. It keeps a rolling hour of aggregate CPU, memory and network throughput measurements in local app storage. Disk Space maps storage using file and folder metadata such as names, paths and sizes in locations macOS allows the app to access. MagicBox keeps the measurements and scan cache on your Mac.

Poppy Velvet

Velvet stores VM disks, settings, console logs and diagnostics on your Mac. You can export diagnostics and choose whether to share them. Software inside a guest can use the network when networking is enabled. If you download an installer or template from a third party, the provider receives connection and request information.

If you connect a VM to a coding agent or other tool, the tool can receive VM information and command output within the access you grant. Its provider governs any further processing by that service.

App downloads and diagnostics

Apple handles App Store transactions and TestFlight distribution. Depending on your settings and how you obtained the app, Apple may share diagnostics or usage reports with Poppy to help improve reliability. You can manage diagnostic sharing in macOS settings. See Apple’s privacy policy. GitHub’s privacy statement applies to direct downloads hosted by GitHub.

Support and email

If you contact us, we use your contact details, message and any files you send to reply and investigate the issue. Google Workspace provides email for the domain. Send only the details needed for your request. We keep correspondence for as long as needed to resolve your request and maintain records needed for legal obligations or claims.

How we use and share personal data

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we use personal data as applicable to provide services you request, pursue our legitimate interests in site security and product support, or comply with legal obligations. Where consent is required for analytics, consent is the applicable basis. We may disclose information if required by law.

Our providers may process data in the United States and other locations described in their policies. Where required for international transfers, applicable safeguards may include adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses. Contact us for information about safeguards for a particular transfer.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, receive a copy, object to or restrict use, withdraw consent, or complain to your data protection authority. Use the contact above to exercise your rights.
